MOUNT FUJI

Mount Fuji in the Morning

I love how the surrounding lakes and forests reflect the mountain, creating a serene and almost magical scene. Every angle seems picture-perfect, and I feel a sense of calm just breathing in the crisp air. It's incredible to see such a majestic symbol of Japan up close—it’s like nature's artwork come to life.

I want to showcase Mount Fuji because the first time I saw it, I thought it was fake or just a drawing—it reminded me of Mount Mayon, but the difference is that Mount Fuji has snow on top. I want to showcase it because I hope to travel to Japan one day and try to climb it if I can.
