INFINITY CASTLE

Infinity Castle

You might be puzzled by this place because there is no "Infinity Castle" in Japan or in real life. This is a fictional location inspired by the anime Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, where the Infinity Castle plays a significant role in the story.

I want to showcase the Infinity Castle because I love Demon Slayer, and I want viewers to know that this fictional story originates from Japanese anime. Also, the Infinity Castle's design and structure surprised me—it is incredibly intricate yet well-organized.
