OSAKA

Osaka in the Morning

I want to experience the calm and refreshing side of Osaka. As the city wakes up, the streets begin to fill with the aroma of freshly cooked takoyaki and okonomiyaki, two of Osaka’s most famous dishes. I imagine walking along the Dotonbori Canal, where the reflections of the early sunlight dance on the water and the neon signs from the night before slowly fade into day.
Osaka’s mornings feel warm and lively, yet peaceful at the same time. I’d love to visit Osaka Castle while the air is still cool, surrounded by cherry trees and the sounds of birds chirping. It’s the perfect place to appreciate the beauty of Japanese history while enjoying the gentle start of a new day.
